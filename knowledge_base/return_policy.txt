Return and Refund Policy

Eligibility
Items may be returned within 30 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging. Software and digital goods are generally non-refundable after download.

Process
1. Request a return in the customer portal under Orders > Select order > Request return.
2. You will receive a return label and reference number.
3. Ship the item to the address on the label.
4. Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days after we receive the item.

Refunds
Refunds are issued to the original payment method. For subscriptions, we prorate unused time when applicable.

Exclusions
Custom or personalized items, perishables, and items marked as final sale cannot be returned.

Contact
For return questions: returns@example.com or use this chat.
