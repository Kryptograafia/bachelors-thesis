FAQ – Customer Support

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Go to Settings > Security > Reset password. You will receive an email with a link to set a new password.

Q: How do I contact support?
A: Use the in-app chat, email support@example.com, or call +1-555-0123 during business hours (9–17 local time).

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfer. Payment is processed at checkout.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: In the dashboard go to Billing > Subscription and click Cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

Q: Where can I find my order history?
A: Log in and open My Account > Orders. You can filter by date and status.
